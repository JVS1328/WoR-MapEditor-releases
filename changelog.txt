- Draw roads by hand: with the Road tool, drag the mouse along the road and let go. The stroke is smoothed and the road is finished when you release. Works in the 2D map and the game view. Clicking points along the road still works as before.
- Draw boundaries and zones by hand: select a map boundary, capture/contention zone or other area, press Draw by hand (or right-click it > Draw the outline by hand), then drag around the new outline. It replaces the old one, and its points can still be dragged, added or removed afterwards. Esc cancels, Ctrl+Z undoes.
