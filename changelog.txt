- Satellite view: water now shows only where it really is. The map's rivers are stored as wide strips that run up the banks under the ground; the editor used to paint all of that as water, so on Harpers Ferry it looked like the river covered the land. Now it matches where the game shows water.
- Clicking water to edit or delete it no longer picks up the river when you click on dry land near it.
- Deleting the map's own water no longer erases riverbank ink from the in-game map pictures.
- Show menu > Water: turn the water off (or back on) in the 2D map, Satellite included, and in the game view.
- Game view: deleted map water disappears right away instead of after reopening.
