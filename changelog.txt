- New in the Map menu: New map from a real place. Pick a 4 km square anywhere on Earth on a map (search for a place, drag the square, turn it), a year up to 1900, and a stock map to take the game modes, areas and spawns from. The editor makes the map for you: the real ground; in the US every tree and boulder where the lidar shows it, elsewhere woods from a global tree-height map; the roads, streams, ponds, fences and stone walls of that year (modern roads are taken out and their ground restored); ground types; the land round the map; and the in-game map pictures redrawn. Buildings are left for you to place, and so are the guns (artillery spawns are kept). Needs the internet and takes a while (Bull Run: about 10-20 minutes); downloads are kept, so the same area again is quicker.
- New in the Map menu: New blank map: a flat 4 km square of grass with the game modes and areas of the stock map you pick.
- Maps made either way have no compass tilt, and open, edit, build and install like any other map. They start unticked for Install / Push.
- The Satellite background pans smoothly at every zoom.
