- New map from a real place: check before you make. "Check roads, woods and fields" (about a minute) draws what was found for your year on the map: click a road, wood or fence to turn it on or off (dashed grey = off; today's roads with no sign of being that old start off), click a field to change its crop (grass, wheat, corn, plowed), and draw missing roads, rail or post fences, stone walls, woods and fields. "Show what was found" hides it all, and "Real map underneath" fades the real map so your choices stand out.
- Trees only where the woods are: lidar trees grow only inside the woods you leave on. A wood of the year with no trees today (a field now, or one you drew) is planted; today's woods are filled in only where the satellite picture shows trees the lidar missed.
- Fields with real crops: in the US each field gets the crop it actually grows in the USDA crop map (corn as corn, wheat and other small grains as wheat, fallow as plowed); elsewhere a guessed July mix. Wheat fields mix standing, partly standing and cut wheat, corn gets its border strip, as on the stock maps.
- Ground colour from the real place: open grass is green, green-yellow or yellow by how green the land is from space (Sentinel-2 cloudless), in the small patches the stock maps use, so a dry place comes out dry with bare dirt and a lush one green.
- The build report (out\realmap\<Map>.json) now lists how long each step took.
