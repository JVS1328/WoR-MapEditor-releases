- Keys and mouse settings (Keys button, top right, or F1): rebind any key (click it, press the new one), saved on your PC. Start from a preset: Standard, Modeller (G / R / S move, rotate, scale; X delete; Shift+D duplicate; ` switches views) or Terrain painter (Alt+H raise, Alt+L lower, Alt+S smooth, Alt+A flatten, Alt+R paint ground, - / + brush size).
- Mouse schemes: on the 2D map, Brush (with a terrain brush, right-drag does the opposite, the wheel sizes the brush, Ctrl+wheel zooms). In the game view, Builder (left-click places and keeps placing, right-click selects, right-click the selection again for the menu).
- Placement previews: a see-through copy of the object follows the mouse in the game view before you place it. The wheel or the mouse thumb buttons turn it (Q / E too, Shift for fine steps), Ctrl+wheel sizes it, and it goes down exactly as shown. The 2D map shows its outline (Q / E or Alt+wheel turn it). Right-click or Esc stops placing.
- Simpler game view: it opens with just the view, the map and three build modes at the bottom: Terrain (1), Pathing (2), Building (3). The hints box is one line; Controls shows the full list.
- The 2D toolbar now reads Select, Gameplay, Terrain, Pathing, Building.
- Q / E turn the selection in the game view, and Ctrl+S saves from it.
