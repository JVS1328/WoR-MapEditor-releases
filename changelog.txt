- Fixed: the game view grew the wrong grass and crops on many ground types: corn patches in open grass, wheat on dirt roads and dead cornstalks among the live corn. Each ground type now grows its own plants. This was only in the editor's game view; built maps were never affected.
- The 2D map zooms in four times closer than before, to pick out small props.

New in 1.0.16, in case you skipped it (fixes for maps with sculpted ground):
- Fixed: a spawn, flag or gun you moved on a map with sculpted ground could be built above or below the ground. It now keeps its height above the ground.
- Fixed: stock roads on sculpted ground could be built too high or too low where the ground change differed along the road.
- Fixed: sculpting the ground near the middle of the map lifted or lowered the far landscape round the whole map. It now stays where it is, and the game view always draws it.
- Fixed: a map whose ground was changed a lot could fail to open in the editor after it was built, because its objects could not be found in the built files.
- Not tested in game yet: these fixes.
