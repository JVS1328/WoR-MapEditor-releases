- New 2D map background: Satellite (bottom left, next to Map / Relief / Map + relief). It shows the map from above as it looks in game: the ground's own colours, fields, woods and roads, with relief shading and contour lines every 3 m that follow your sculpting.
- Trees, fences and stone walls, rocks and buildings are drawn as their real models seen from above, with soft shadows: tree tops, roofs, snake-rail fences that zig-zag like in game. They stay smooth at any zoom and sharp when you zoom in close.
- Buildings are cut where they go under the ground, so earth ramps (like the Roulette barn's) and the ground modelled around houses show only the part you see in game.
- The first time you open Satellite it draws every model from above; the status line shows how far along it is. On a map you haven't opened in the game view yet this can take a while.

New in 1.0.19, in case you skipped it:
- Build now tells you whether your map works on the server alone. Some edits need nothing on the players' side: moving spawn points and guns, changing boundaries, new ticket counts. Others stop players without your files from joining at all: added or deleted guns, spawn points and capture points, added or removed areas, added or deleted breakable objects. After each Build the message either says the build works on the server alone, or lists exactly what would keep players out.
- The README's "server only" table is updated with what was tested in game and what the game's network code allows.
