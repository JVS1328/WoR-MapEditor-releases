- Build menu: Export as .zip to share. It builds, then makes a .zip of the mod and shows it in Explorer. To use it: open the zip and drag everything in it into War of Rights\Assets.
- Game view: the shadows and foliage toggles turn back on again. Looking around with the right mouse button used to switch both off.
- The Build message names the map that was built, even if you open another map while it builds.
