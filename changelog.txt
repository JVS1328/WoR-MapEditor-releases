- New in Show (bottom left): Sprites. Guns and limbers are drawn as the real pieces seen from above (12-pdr Napoleon, the CSA Napoleon, 10-pdr Parrott, 3-inch Ordnance Rifle, limbers), and every spawn shows a USA or CSA infantryman, cavalryman or gunner, picked by its team and unit type, on a disc of its team's colour. They face the way the object faces, stay readable when you zoom out, and names keep a white halo so they read over the sprites.
- Roads now get a shoulder like the game's own roads: under the road its ground type (Dirt3 by default), and past each edge a strip of a second ground type you choose (No vegetation grass by default, 2 m wide), so grass and crops don't grow right up to the road. Both types and the shoulder width are in the Road panel, in the 2D map and the game view. Deleting a road takes the shoulder back too. Roads drawn before this update keep no shoulder: redraw them or paint it with the ground brush.

New in 1.0.22, in case you skipped it:
- New 2D map background: Satellite (bottom left, next to Map / Relief / Map + relief). It shows the map from above as it looks in game: the ground's own colours, fields, woods and roads, with relief shading and contour lines every 3 m, and trees, fences, rocks and buildings drawn as their real models seen from above, with soft shadows.
