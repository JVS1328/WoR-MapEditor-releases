- Props that come with their parts: a static caisson, limber or gun carriage now comes with its wheels, set exactly as the game assembles them. An artillery embankment (sandbag or plain earth) comes with its log revetment and gabions, laid out like the stock Harpers Ferry gun positions. Works when placing, along a Fence line (a line of dressed embankments) and with the Paint brush; the placement previews show the parts too. Untick "With parts" to place just the bare model.
- Groups: a placed caisson or embankment stays together as one group. Click any piece to select the whole thing, then move, rotate, copy, paste, duplicate or delete it in one go. Group any objects yourself with Ctrl+G (or Group in the right-click menu), and split them again with Ctrl+Shift+G (Ungroup). Drop to ground and Align to slope keep a group together (a caisson lands on its wheels).
