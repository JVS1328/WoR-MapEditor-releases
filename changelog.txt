- Water tool (W, or 4 in the game view): draw creeks, rivers, ponds and lakes. Creeks and rivers: drag or click a line along them; they run downhill whichever way you draw them, and the channel is dug with sloping banks and a riverbed. Ponds and lakes: draw around them; the water sits just under the lowest ground around the edge. Pick the width and depth.
- Edit any water, yours or the map's own: click it, drag its squares to reshape it or drag the water to move it, double-click a square to remove it or the line to add one, and change width and depth. Moving water fills its old bed back in and digs the new one. Ctrl+Z undoes each change.
- Delete water: yours (its bed is filled back in) or the map's (a whole river, or Shift+click for one piece).
- Water looks like the map's own: black ink on the paper map, water colour on Satellite (where the map's own creeks now show too), and in the game view. Build draws new water on the in-game map pictures and removes deleted map water from them.
