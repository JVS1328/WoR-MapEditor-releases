- Satellite opens much faster: the trees, fences, rocks and buildings drawn from above are now kept between sessions instead of redrawn every launch (Antietam: about 17 s down to 3 s). They are drawn once more after this update, then kept. Your edits still show at once: only how each model looks from above is kept, never where things are.
