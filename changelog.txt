- Work as a team (Build menu): share a map through a private GitHub repo, all inside the editor. Sign in with a GitHub token (the dialog links to the page that makes one), Start a team to create the repo, invite teammates by GitHub username; they accept in their own Team dialog. Sync brings in everyone's changes and sends yours, item by item. If you and someone else changed the same thing, it asks: Keep mine or Take theirs, and Show draws both on the map (yours orange, theirs blue). History lets you go back to any sync. Not yet tested with two people on a real repo: tell us how it goes.
- Team kits: select objects in the game view, Selection > Share as team kit, and everyone finds them in Place > Team kits.
- Auto-save and backups (Build menu > Backups and auto-save): saves every 2, 5, 10 or 15 minutes (5 by default) and keeps your last 30 saves of each map to restore.
- Selected objects in the game view get a bright outline.
- Select filter (top of the game view): choose whether a click or box picks natural things (trees, rocks, plants), man-made objects, or what you placed. Turn off natural to stop grabbing foliage.
- Local axes: the move / rotate gizmo can follow the object's own axes instead of the world's.
- The inspector's position, height, facing, pitch, roll and scale drag like sliders: drag left or right, Shift for finer, Ctrl for bigger steps, click to type. One drag is one undo.
- Drill Camp: spawn points can be selected and edited. The ropes on bayonet dummies and telegraph poles follow when you move them and go when you delete them.
- Removed the RCON instructions: War of Rights has no RCON.
